# Operating and safety contract

Canonical definitions are in schemas/ and catalog/owners.json within this package. The runner loads only those local schemas and never resolves a schema supplied by evidence. Organization-controlled trust pins the verifier bundle (runtime, schemas, catalog and tests), approved profiles, issuers, roles, owner scopes, validity, evidence cutoff and revocations. Obtain its digest independently of submitted evidence. Protect it, the state DB and signing credentials from the assessed process with host permissions and separate execution identities.

All 13 owner disciplines remain visible. Preliminary discovery/threat/regulatory/data/vendor work determines the test scope; final challenge evaluates integrated evidence. The planning stages are dependency guidance, not proof work occurred. Each case defines observable harm predicates and profile-supplied bounds. The collector must retain direct evidence for its observations. A boolean assertion alone is insufficient enterprise collection quality even when a verifier accepts its shape.

The immutable subject binds source/build/config/model/prompt/tool/corpus/memory/consent/enforcement identities plus tenant, environment and workflow. A changed subject requires a new run; never rewrite a live manifest. Profile thresholds must be approved before tests and cannot weaken built-in non-waivable controls. The provided local-insurance profile uses synthetic thresholds only.

Risk states preserve observed versus hypothesized behavior, missing versus untestable evidence, technical treatment, and exception acceptance separately. Unknown exposure blocks a required bounded operation. Every unresolved applicable requirement stays in the denominator. Waivers require authenticated executive authority, current compensating evidence, bounded exposure and independent challenge. They cannot cover the built-in security floor. Different organization-unit strings are useful enforcement inputs only when the organization independently administers those attributes; they are not proof of organizational reality.

The model/provider evidence expiry in every envelope is rechecked before decisions. Capability, model route, prompts, tools, memory, permissions, data/jurisdiction, monitoring or evaluator changes invalidate the affected subject or evidence. Schedule finite reassessment ticks using the organization's scheduler. Review primary model/security/deprecation evidence on the approved cadence; do not automatically promote newer models or weaken tests.

For a suspected compromised signer/collector, revoke its issuer or invocation and move evidence_not_before to the last trusted checkpoint where appropriate. Rerun affected decisions and request product restrictions through authorized adapters; acknowledge actual enforcement separately. Exported historical decisions do not constitute current authorization.
